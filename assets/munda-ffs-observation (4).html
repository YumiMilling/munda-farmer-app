Standard rates — quick reference
Fertiliser: 1 bottle top per plant = 200 kg/ha · 1 bottle top per 2 plants = 100 kg/ha
Compost: 1 × 20L bucket per plot = 5 t/ha
Green manure: 25 kg fresh weight per plot = 6,600 kg/ha
Lime: 3.75 kg per plot = 1 t/ha
Standard rate used: 100 kg/ha (closer to country average than recommended 200 kg)
